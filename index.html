Grounded in Cameroon. Built for lasting business growth.

EJAR GROUP SARL is a business compliance and growth partner based in Douala-Bonabéri, Cameroon, serving entrepreneurs, SMEs and organisations across Douala, Buea and Yaoundé.

Across Cameroon, businesses don't usually fail for lack of customers or effort. They fail because of what nobody is watching: books that are not kept, taxes filed late, cash that leaves without control, and decisions taken without reliable numbers. EJAR Group exists to close that gap, so that businesses can grow on solid ground.

Every engagement starts with a diagnostic. We take the time to understand your business before recommending anything. From there, we work to written procedures, review every deliverable before it reaches you, and treat every client file as confidential.

EJAR Group was founded by Raymond Agbor, a finance and audit professional and the author of four books for entrepreneurs, who works across Africa and Europe. It was co-founded by Minang William, a banker in Cameroon who holds an MBA in Finance.

We don't just fix problems. We build the systems, the numbers and the people that keep them from coming back.

EJAR Group. We Succeed Together.
