About Us

Building businesses that last. EJAR GROUP SARL is a business compliance and growth partner based in Douala-Bonabéri, Cameroon, serving entrepreneurs, SMEs and organisations across Douala, Buea and Yaoundé.

Why EJAR Exists

Across Cameroon, businesses don't usually fail for lack of customers or effort. They fail because of what nobody is watching: books that are not kept, taxes filed late, cash that leaves without control, and decisions taken without reliable numbers. EJAR Group exists to close that gap, so that businesses can grow on solid ground.

What We Do

Our work is organised around four pillars: Business Compliance keeps the business legal and protected; Financial Management gives owners a clear picture of where the business stands; Business Growth helps the business scale beyond its founder; and Capacity Building gives teams the skills to sustain what we build together.

How We Work

Every engagement starts with a diagnostic. We take the time to understand your business before recommending anything. From there, we work to written procedures, review every deliverable before it reaches you, and treat every client file as confidential.
